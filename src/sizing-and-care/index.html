Sizing & Care

Choose the size you will actually wear.

Relaxed pieces still need good measurements. Use this page before asking about availability.

Sizing notes

Compare garment measurements with a similar piece you already like. For relaxed dresses and sets, pay attention to bust, waist, hip, rise, inseam and strap length. If you are between sizes, decide whether you prefer a closer shape or a breezier fit.

Care notes

Wash cool when possible, avoid harsh heat, reshape textured pieces while damp and let linen or gauze keep some natural movement. The goal is not perfection. The goal is clothing that still feels good after real life.
